# Stage 4 -- offline starvation estimate

Generated by `scripts/estimate_starvation.py` over `run_clean.jsonl`. No model call, no network, no randomness.

Two scripted analyses bracket what the analysis step could return, and `kernel_verdict` -- the pure half -- resolves each. Both use `certainty="definitive"`, the strictest claim available.

| pass | what the analysis says | CLEAR | RESTRICT | UNSAFE |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| optimistic | every admissible item `supports` | 50 | 0 | 0 |
| pessimistic | every admissible item `irrelevant` | 0 | 50 | 0 |

Over **50** clean scenarios.

**The optimistic pass is CLEAR for every clean scenario (50/50).** The verdict logic does not starve: SUPPORT_ONLY evidence alone authorizes a definitive claim, which is what Stage 3's 325-of-447 demotion rate requires of it.

**The pessimistic pass is RESTRICT for 50/50 scenarios**, every one via `NO_ADMISSIBLE_SUPPORT`. That is the share of the clean arm whose pass-through rests entirely on the analysis step finding at least one supporting link. It is the quantity a live run measures and this script cannot.

Scenarios with **no admissible evidence at all**: 0. (Those cannot be CLEAR on either pass, and are counted above rather than excluded.)

This is a bound on the verdict logic, **not a prediction of live behaviour**. The clean pass-through rate is unmeasured until `scripts/run_kernel_offline.py` is run against a live model.
